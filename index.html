01 — ABOUT RAS

More than a club.
A robotics ecosystem.

IEEE Robotics and Automation Society at VIT Chennai brings together students who design, build, experiment and collaborate across the world of robotics and intelligent systems.

From autonomous systems and embedded electronics to artificial intelligence, computer vision and automation, RAS provides a platform for students to turn engineering concepts into working systems.

Our community connects different technical disciplines through projects, workshops, research and hands-on experimentation — encouraging students to learn by building.

Whether it is developing a robot, working with sensors, designing intelligent algorithms or solving real-world engineering problems, RAS is where ideas move from concepts to prototypes.

03 — PROJECTS

Our Research Projects

Turning ideas into intelligent systems — real-world solutions through robotics, automation and AI.

PROJECT / 01

Heart Disease Risk Prediction App

This application is a machine learning-based diagnostic tool designed to predict the likelihood of heart disease by analyzing various clinical parameters provided by the user, such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and other relevant health indicators.

Machine Learning | Web Application →
PROJECT / 02

Hospital Locator & Navigator

The Real-Time Hospital Locator and Navigation System is a web-based tool designed to quickly identify nearby hospitals and provide the shortest route from a user-defined location. Unlike general-purpose platforms like Google Maps, it is optimized specifically for medical emergencies, offering a fast, focused, and user-friendly interface to assist during critical situations.

Web Application | Real-Time Navigation →
PROJECT / 03

Line Follower Robot

A line follower robot is an autonomous robot that follows a specific path, usually a black line on a white surface. It uses infrared sensors to detect the line and adjusts its movement accordingly. The robot continuously scans the path and makes real-time decisions to stay on track. It demonstrates concepts of automation, control systems, and robotics.
